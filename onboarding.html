No restructure

Keystone maps your existing fields into one consistent model, so your systems of record stay authoritative and nothing has to be rebuilt. That is why a pilot is measured in weeks, not quarters.

Our AI understands your product structure. It recognises what is a product, a component, a variant or SKU, and a batch, so each piece of information goes to the right place and is reused properly across every product that shares it.

  • A component used in twenty products is stored once and linked twenty times, not copied
  • Variant and SKU attributes stay with the variant; shared specifications stay with the product
  • Batch-level facts, such as origin or test results, stay with the batch they describe
  • Certifications and documents are attached at the level they actually cover, then reused wherever that component or material appears

The long game

The alternative to a pilot is buying a segregated tool for each new regulation, which only deepens the data fragmentation and the cost. A pilot proves the opposite: one foundation that every future rule reuses. Waiting, or adding point tools, simply raises the bill and spreads the problem across every team.
