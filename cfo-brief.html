The pattern

The pain: A tool for DPP, another for EUDR, another for LCA, each with its own copy of the same product data. The copies drift, and someone is paid to reconcile them before every filing.

Where the money actually goes

  • Staff hours reconciling conflicting data across tools, not producing anything
  • Evidence packs rebuilt per market and per retailer instead of reused
  • Audit and claims risk when a number cannot be traced to its source
  • Licence spend that rises with every regulation, with no consolidated view

What to measure

  • Time per filing, today versus on one record
  • Number of tools holding product data, and how much they overlap
  • Staff hours spent reconciling, per quarter
  • Scope 3 exposure from capital goods, which count in full the year you buy them
