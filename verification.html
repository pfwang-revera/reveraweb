What we verify

Independent verification for projects and products across materials, compliance, and performance claims.

Project Verification:
Real Estate & Interiors

Independent assessment of as-installed materials.

  • What we verify: Documented material in retail, office, hospitality, pop-ups, experiential spaces.
  • What we quantify: Carbon, Circularity, Health, Operational data (space/system level).
  • How we close the gap: Distilled benchmarks & proxy data for consistency when supplier data is incomplete.
  • What we output: verified results, findings (major/minor/obs), CAPA plan, data pack for reuse.

Operational Sustainability:
Air | Energy | Water | Waste

Turn day-to-day operations into verified performance.

  • Air (world-leading RESET standard): real-time, sensor-based IAQ verification (continuous evidence, QA rules, alerting & triage).
  • Energy: metering evidence, baselines, reduction tracking; normalization by hours/area/occupancy.
  • Water: consumption quantity; reuse/filtration evidence.
  • Waste: weight quantity, sorting & diversion rates; downstream recycler documentation (can be linked to embodied circularity).
  • Landlord common-area apportioning: CAM splits (pro-rata, sub-meter, blended), shared equipment allocation, open/closed-hour adjustments, multi-tenant rollups through Tenant First Program.
  • Reduction: Credible carbon reduction plan with qualified offset credits to transition.

Product Verification:
Compliance & Unified Truth

Impartial verification for product claims and market access.

  • Technical files (GPSR/CE/UKCA), chemical evidence (REACH/RoHS), packaging EPR
  • Certificates/COAs validity, chain-of-custody & mass balance
  • Health declarations (e.g., green chemistry, allergen, VOC, red list)
  • Optional: sync verified evidence to Product Keystone
