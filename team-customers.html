Keystone can help brands turn product knowledge into reasons to choose, and give customers reasons to stay connected after buying. The shared BOM and evidence platform is the foundation; the opportunity is making that information useful at each buying and ownership moment.

Two outcomes are worth exploring on the customer side: help people choose, and help owners get more value from the product. These are opportunities to test, rather than claims that Keystone already delivers every feature. (The professional-buyer and specifier path lives with Keystone for Sales.)

Opportunities to test

These experiences have a practical foundation: GS1 guidance for brands describes product-linked access to information and services, while the European Commission identifies care, repair and spare-parts information among potential DPP uses. Neither establishes that engagement automatically produces sales.

For consumer engagement, lead with usefulness

A passport that opens with a long compliance record may attract little repeat use. A product page organised around customer questions has a clearer purpose:

Before purchaseAfter purchase
Is it right for my space?How do I assemble and care for it?
Which finish should I choose?Which replacement part fits?
What supports this product claim?Can it be repaired or refurbished?
Where can I see or buy it?How can I pass it on or resell it?

Keep essential information accessible without registration. Offer registration when it provides a clear benefit, such as saving the product details or receiving relevant service updates.

A starting example, and the next experiment

&tradition’s published showroom story shows how Keystone-powered passports made information about materials, components, suppliers and production accessible alongside selected designs. It documents the experience, rather than a measured sales uplift. Studio 9’s &tradition story.

A good first consumer test is an owner-care page: connect one product family to care and spare-part guidance. Measure successful self-service, support contacts and relevant parts enquiries. Use comparable products or a before-and-after baseline, and avoid attributing every subsequent sale to a passport interaction.

Keystone makes the product information you collect useful wherever it can help someone choose or care for the product. Which of those moments is causing your furniture customers the most difficulty today?
