What you collect for everything

Before any risk tier applies, every relevant product needs the same evidence, down to the plot it came from.

  • Commodity and the products derived from it (for furniture and lighting, wood and wood-based parts)
  • Geolocation of every plot of production, as coordinates or polygons for larger plots
  • Country of production, quantity, and date or time range of production
  • Supplier and the evidence that production was legal in that country

Then the work scales with the risk

Low-risk countries

  • Simplified due diligence: collect the information and confirm risk is no more than negligible
  • No full risk assessment or mitigation step required, so sourcing here is the lightest path

Standard-risk countries

  • Full due diligence: information, then a documented risk assessment, then mitigation where needed
  • You must show risk is negligible before the goods go on the market

High-risk countries

  • Full due diligence plus enhanced scrutiny, and authorities check a far higher share of these operators
  • Expect to justify every plot and legality document, so weak upstream data shows up fastest here

AI that reads what suppliers actually send

  • Deciphers documentation, invoices and certifications across languages and formats, including handwriting
  • Automatically matches upstream and downstream suppliers and checks quantities, so the chain reconciles
  • Flags gaps and inconsistencies for review, so a weak file is caught before submission
  • Cuts reliance on expensive manual expert review and reduces legal exposure, without removing your final sign-off
