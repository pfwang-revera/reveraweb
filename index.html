What we offer

Two path - one truth

1) Software: Product Keystone

Unify product data. Prove what matters.

  • Enable LCA, GPSR, EUDR, FSC, EPR compliance
  • Certification management
  • Supplier data submissions
  • Batch ↔ PO x supplier source traceability
  • ESPR-compliant DPP; CSRD-ready data model
  • API synchronization

2) Verification

Independent assessment of projects and operational performance and products.

  • As-installed material verification with documented sources
  • Embodied carbon at system levels with high quality proxy data to bridge any gap
  • Circularity: disassembly, take-back & recycling solution
  • Health: material scoring and screening, exposure relevance, better alternatives
  • Operational data including air, energy, water and waste in a bundled offering
  • Premium advisory when you need to push to frontier and set internal standards, develop unique design for disassembly program and more
