Questions & resources

Get a quick answer, or go deeper.

The questions buyers ask most, each with a link to the full detail when you need it.

Is Keystone just another software tool?
No. Keystone is the one governed foundation that replaces the tool-per-regulation sprawl. Instead of a new silo for each rule, you build one data model every team and every future regulation reuses, which lowers cost and headache over time.Go deeper: Read the compliance data whitepaper
How hard is onboarding for IT?
Keystone maps your existing ERP and PIM fields into one consistent model and syncs both ways, so your systems of record are untouched and there is no restructure. You can start through API and MCP, AI-assisted file import, or manual entry, and onboarding is measured in weeks.Go deeper: See the three onboarding routes
Will it change how my teams work?
No workflow rip-up. Teams keep the tools they use, and templates, terminology and views are tailored by brand and by user so data collection matches how people already work.Go deeper: See the daily work by team
How does Keystone make my work more efficient?
It removes repeated collection. One supplier request, chained upstream to whoever holds the data, collects a fact once; that answer is then reused across every product, filing and passport that needs it, instead of being chased again.Go deeper: How supply chain requests work
Do you offer verification?
Yes. Independent, RESET-standard verification of embodied carbon and other claims turns an assertion into documented, third-party evidence, which writes back into the Keystone record.Go deeper: About independent verification
What is the return on investment?
Lower cost per regulation, far less reconciliation, and reused evidence on the cost side; and on the revenue side, consumer-facing Digital Product Passports that can drive aftersales, spare-parts sales and cross-sell.Go deeper: Open the ROI model
Does Keystone replace our ERP or PIM?
No. It syncs both ways with ERP, PIM, PLM and DAM through MCP and API, and becomes the single governed source for compliance and sustainability data on top of them.Go deeper: Integration & security
Which regulations does Keystone support?
ESPR and the Digital Product Passport, EUDR with submission to TRACES, PPWR, GPSR, REACH, RoHS, POP, CE, LVD, EMC, EN standards, the EU Battery Regulation, the EU Cosmetics Regulation, CSRD-aligned reporting and LCA.Go deeper: Where to start: DPP & EUDR checklist
