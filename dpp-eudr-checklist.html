Start with the one that is biting first.

DPP and EUDR are different problems and a single combined checklist helps with neither. DPP depends on your industry; EUDR depends on where your inputs come from. Pick the path that matches your deadline.

A wave of regulation is arriving at once.

ESPR and the Digital Product Passport, EUDR, PPWR, GPSR, the EU Battery Regulation and CSRD are landing across the next few years, and most teams are not ready. The data each one needs takes months to collect from suppliers. Starting now is the difference between a calm rollout and a last-minute scramble.
