About Keystone

Keystone is the product data and compliance platform for every team that touches the product, with independent verification for products and physical spaces. We help brands turn scattered product data into one record every team can trust.

What we do

One platform for compliance and sustainability, backed by an expert verification team. Software keeps product data current and synced to the systems you already run; verification makes the claims defensible.

About Revera Limited

Keystone is built by Revera Limited, an Irish company. Revera brings together two sides of one mission: the Keystone software platform that organises product and compliance data, and an independent verification practice that assesses products and physical spaces to recognised standards, including RESET, for embodied carbon, circularity, health and more. The software keeps the data current; verification makes the claims defensible.

Where we are

Keystone is built by Revera Limited in Dublin, Shanghai, Paris and Montreal. We work with partners across design, apparel, furniture, lighting and personal care.
